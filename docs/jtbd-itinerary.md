# RWC itinerary creation: jobs to be done

Nov 12, 2026 · @Monique

These are 32 jobs people are trying to do when they plan an itinerary around the Rugby World Cup. Jobs T1 to T16 cover choosing teams and tickets, which decide what the planner shows. Jobs 1 to 16 cover planning the itinerary, grouped by stage. They come from DNSW research figures, user interviews, workshop outputs and the existing RWC concepts.

Each job has an evidence label:

- **Research:** a DNSW research figure. Before sharing, check each one comes from the Event Traveller or BEM AI Tools research, as only those two can be called research.
- **Thought starter:** interviews, workshops, concepts or industry conversations. These are not stated as fact.
- **Gap:** no evidence yet.

## Main job

When I'm travelling in NSW for an event, I want to fit the things I'd like to do around my fixed plans, so I can make the most of a short trip without the stress of organising it.

## Stage 0: Choosing teams and tickets

This is the first step, and what people choose here decides which matches the planner shows. It has to work for people who support one team, several teams, or none.

### Supporting one team

| # | Job | Evidence |

### What each choice changes in the planner

| Choice | Matches shown | Highlighted | Planning starts from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One team | That team's matches | Matches with a ticket | Ticketed matches, then NSW matches |
| More than one team | All chosen teams' matches, shared matches shown once | Matches with a ticket | Ticketed matches, then NSW matches |
| No team | Matches the person picks by date or place | Matches with a ticket | The dates of their trip |
| Skipped | None until they add some | Nothing | The dates of their trip, or what they save |

## Stage 1: Collecting

| # | Job | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | When I find places I might like, wherever I see them, I want to save them quickly in one place without signing up, so I don't lose them. | **Thought starter:** people save across TikTok, Instagram, Notion and Maps, and collect before ordering. **Research:** only 9% plan on destination sites. |
| 2 | When I'm still deciding where to go, I want to save towns and areas, not only businesses, so I can work out where to base myself. | **Thought starter:** people save areas while exploring and businesses once the trip is real. |
| 3 | When I've decided to go at short notice, I want a ready-made plan I can copy and change, so I can plan quickly. | **Research:** 70% decide and book within two months. **Thought starter:** interest in sample itineraries. |

## Stage 2: Ordering and timing

| # | Job | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | When I have a match or booking I can't move, I want to see what fits in the time around it, so I don't put it at risk. | **Thought starter:** the "detour and nights" concept. |
| 5 | When I'm looking at places to go, I want to know how far they are and how long it takes to get there, so I can tell whether a plan is realistic. | **Thought starter:** the most common gap in interviews and workshop maps. **Research:** getting there is a barrier for 20%. |
| 6 | When I'm deciding how long to stay, I want my list of things to do to show how many nights I need, so I book the right number. | **Thought starter:** a list of things to do decides the nights. **Research:** 71% of event trips are 1 or 2 nights. |
| 7 | When my trip covers more than one area, I want to see whether to stay in one place or move along a route, so I don't waste time travelling. | **Thought starter:** one base for compact areas, moving on for longer routes. |
| 8 | When I have spare time around the event, I want to find somewhere to eat or shop nearby, so I can fill the gap easily. | **Research:** dining out 71%, shopping 54%. |
| 9 | When money matters, I want a rough idea of what a plan costs, so I can decide if it's worth it. | **Research:** cost is the top barrier at 43%. **Thought starter:** spreadsheets are used to track cost. No concept covers this yet. |
| 10 | When I don't want a strict schedule, I want to plan only as much as I like and leave gaps, so the trip doesn't feel over-planned. | **Thought starter:** people plan with very different amounts of detail, and some get decision fatigue. |

## Stage 3: Agreeing and taking it with me

| # | Job | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | When I'm going with other people, I want to share the plan where we already talk, so we can agree on it without anyone signing up. | **Research:** coordination is a barrier, and travel companions motivate 33%. **Thought starter:** groups organise in WhatsApp (industry conversation). |
| 12 | When my plan is ready, I want to move it into the apps I already use, such as maps, calendar, notes or a spreadsheet, so I don't have to type it out again. | **Thought starter:** addresses copied into Maps, timings put into a calendar. |
| 13 | When I use an AI assistant to help plan, I want details I can check and trust, so I'm not caught out by wrong information. | **Research:** 41% have used AI to plan a holiday, but only 20% trust it. |

## Things that change how hard the job is, not the job itself

- Budget, luxury, families, groups and travelling without a car.
- Trip length: one night, a weekend, or several days.
- Whether the trip is built around a fixed plan, such as a match, or is open.
- Whether people plan alone or for a group.
