When to use

A short label that says what something is, such as whether a match is in NSW. Tags are not buttons and do nothing when tapped.

Variants

  • Solid: the main state, "In NSW". In The Third Half, volt with ink text.
  • Outline: the other state, naming the city ("Perth"). Dashed in the Wireframe, an ink line in The Third Half.

The label carries the meaning, not the colour.

Content

  • One or two words. In The Third Half, Mono caps, never a sentence.
