Stack only over a full-screen layer

A sheet opened from a full-screen layer (the open plan, a full-screen map) slides up over it, and the layer steps back. The layer counts as the first level, so there are never more than two. A third sheet replaces the second.

Motion

  • The sheet slides up; the layer behind moves back (94% from its top centre, raised 10px, dimmed), in one motion with one timing. A strip of the layer shows above.
  • Reduced motion: no slide or scale, both states appear at once.

Closing

  • The cross, Escape (top sheet only), a tap on the strip of the layer above, or a drag on the grabber past a third of the way (or a flick).
  • The layer comes forward and focus returns to the control that opened the sheet.

Example

Save & share at the foot of the plan opens the Save and share sheet stacked over the plan. See it in the Pick your team prototype.
