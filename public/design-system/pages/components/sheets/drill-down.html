Inside a sheet, always drill down

Any link to more detail inside a sheet (a venue, a match, a place, an idea, the next step of a choice) slides across inside the same sheet, with a back arrow.

Tap a venue in Where to watch: the venue slides in from the right inside the same sheet.

Open a second sheet on top of the first.

Height holds

A sheet keeps its height while drilling down. Each level fills the height the sheet opened at, so only the content moves, never the sheet's edge. A taller level scrolls inside it. Going back keeps the same height. The height changes only when the sheet first opens or closes.

A short venue page fills the sheet's height, with space below.

Shrink the sheet to the venue's short content. It reads as a jump.

Motion

  • A drill-down pushes in from the right; going back slides away to the right.
  • Reduced motion: the level swaps in place.

The same thing opens the same content

A venue opens the same details whether it is tapped on the page, in the open plan, on a map or in another sheet.

Escape

Escape goes back one level, not out of the whole sheet. For example, Escape on an idea's details goes back to the list of ideas.
