Sheets open where the person is, so the page does not move. Inside a sheet, more detail drills down; over a full-screen layer, a sheet stacks.

Anatomy

  1. Grabber: tap or drag to go back a step.
  2. Close cross: top right, 16px from the edge, on every sheet and layer. Closes the whole sheet.
  3. Back arrow: top left, only below the first level.
  4. Body: scrolls inside the sheet.
  5. Pinned bottom row (optional): the sheet's main action.

Controls

ControlDoes
Close crossCloses the whole sheet, drill levels and all. Over a layer, closes the sheet and leaves the layer open.
Back arrowBack one level.
EscapeBack one step; closes the sheet at its first level.
Grabber tap, or drag past a third of the height, or a flickBack one step; closes at the first level. A shorter drag springs back.
The dark area behindCloses everything.

Every grabber works. Nothing draws a grabber that does nothing.

Focus and announcements

  • Focus moves into the sheet when it opens and stays there (Tab cycles inside the top sheet).
  • Focus returns to whatever opened the level just closed.
  • Announce "[name], details opened." and "Back to [title]."

Where it lives in the code

Sheets are built in concepts-third-half.js (pmPush for drilling, tpPicker.stackOpen for stacking) with .cn-pp, .tp-pm, .tp-stack and .cn-grabber in shared.css. A live example is planned for version 0.2; until then, see them in the Pick your team prototype.
