When to use

  • The main action of a screen area: "Pick your teams", "Add to your plan" in a place's details.
  • Inside sheets, as the action that completes the sheet's job.

When not to use

  • Several equal actions side by side: use chips or text links.
  • Adding an item from a card in a list: use the add button.

Anatomy

  1. Container: --c-btn fill, --radius-pill, 36px min height.
  2. Optional icon, 16px, before the label.
  3. Label: --fs-md, --fw-bold, --c-on-dark.

As a toggle

With .tp-pm-add and aria-pressed, the pill adds and removes. The icon changes from add to added and the label from "Add to your plan" to "Added to your plan". Pressing it again removes.

Accessibility

  • A <button type="button">, never a link styled as a button, unless it goes to another page.
  • As a toggle, aria-pressed carries the state; hidden text names the item.
