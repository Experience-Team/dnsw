Choosing a button

ButtonUse forHow many

Brand buttonThe one colourway moment.One per screen

Shared rules

  • 36px high, at least 24px to tap. See accessibility.
  • Labels say what happens and sit on one line from 320 to 390px in every brand.
  • A button that moves the page says where it goes, and may end with a right arrow.
  • Pressed: a brief scale to 96 to 97%. Focus: a 2px ring in --c-focus.
