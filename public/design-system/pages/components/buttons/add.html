When to use

On a product card, a match row or a list item, to add that one item to the plan. The same button shows that it has been added, and removes it when pressed again.

When not to use

For the main action of a sheet or screen: use the pill button.

Anatomy

  1. Container: an outlined pill, --bw-2 in --c-line-ink, 72px min width, 36px high.
  2. Icon: the add or added circle from the icon set.
  3. Label: "Add", then "Added".

States

  • Off: outline on --c-surface.
  • On (Added): filled --c-btn with --c-on-dark text and a tick.

Behaviour

  • The whole card or row is not the button: the add button sits above it (z-index: 1) so the row can open details.
  • Adding something that lands out of view (the plan, Where to watch) is confirmed with a toast, unless the change already shows where the person is.

Accessibility

  • aria-pressed carries the state.
  • The visible label is short; hidden text says the whole thing: "Add Bondi to Coogee walk to your plan", then "Added: Bondi to Coogee walk. Press to remove".
