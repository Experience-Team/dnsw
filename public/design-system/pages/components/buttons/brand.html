When to use

For one action per screen that should carry the brand: in The Third Half, Save & share at the foot of the plan, and Pick your teams in the hero. They are rarely on screen together.

When not to use

For more than one action on a screen. A second colourway moment weakens both.

Brand rules

  • Wireframe: an outlined pill, like a secondary button.
  • The Third Half: the colourway fill with ink text. Chalk on a colourway fails contrast below 24px, so text stays ink.

Known gap

The brand button is styled by its id (#tp-tl-share), not a class, so it cannot be reused yet. Version 0.2 gives it a class.
