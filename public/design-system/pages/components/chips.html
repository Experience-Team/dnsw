Single choice

Pick one from a short set, such as a day. Picking one replaces the last, so exactly one is on.

Several at once

Pick any number. Follows the choosing several things pattern:

  • "Anything" replaces the specific picks, and picking a specific one turns "Anything" off. The two never mix.
  • Clear all appears once something is picked and clears everything in one action.

Anatomy

  1. Container: 36px high pill, --bw-2 line in --c-line-strong.
  2. Label: --fs-sm, --fw-semi, on one line.

States

  • Off: outline on --c-surface.
  • On: --c-btn fill with --c-on-dark text, and the line in --c-line-ink.

Accessibility

  • Buttons with aria-pressed, in a group with a label.
  • A row that scrolls sideways keeps every chip reachable by Tab.
