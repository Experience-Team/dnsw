Widths

  • Phone prototypes are judged at 390 by 844, and must hold from 320 to 390 wide.
  • Text meant to sit on one line (a tab label, a slogan line, a button) holds on one line in every brand and font from 320 to 390px. The wireframe's system font is wider than The Third Half's condensed face, so check both.
  • Page wireframes use a 1280px container with 32px sides.

Spacing rhythm

All space comes from the 4px scale. Distance shows relationship:

RelationshipSpace
Inside a component4 to 16px
Parts of one family24px
Between families, and before each section's rule48px
After the heroThe largest break on the page

Pairs that belong together sit closer than their neighbours, for example a slogan and its event line.

Separate the hand of cards, the map and the match cards with 48px so they read as three things.

Draw boxes or rules to group things that space could group.

Content fits its window

  • Nothing meant to be seen is cut off by its container at rest: a card corner, a shadow, a lifted item.
  • Size containers from the content's real extremes in every state (dragged to either end, picked, lifted, focused), and measure the result.
  • Clip only the sides that are meant to run on, such as a carousel running off the edge. A brief lift may rise out of its box.
  • Space above and below content is even.

Optical alignment

Judge centring, padding and indents from the rendered pixels, not only the numbers: fonts and their metrics differ between brands and devices. For example, the Add buttons measured as centred but read low in one font, so they carry a small per-brand nudge (--add-nudge).
