Target

WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page and prototype. Not AAA: where AA and AAA differ, build to AA and do not pad beyond it.

Target size

  • Every control is at least 24 by 24px to tap (2.5.8). A smaller visible control gets an invisible target around it.
  • 36px is the usual button and chip height. Do not pad tap areas out to 44px.
  • Links inside running text are sized by their line, which AA allows.

Contrast

  • Text: 4.5:1. Large text (24px, or 19px bold) and the edges of controls: 3:1.
  • The colour page shows each role's contrast in the chosen brand.
  • Where a brand colour fails as small text, the brand sets an ink for it. For example, The Third Half's orange is 3.2:1 on chalk, so small orange text uses --c-accent-ink (ink) and orange is kept for fills and large type.

Not colour alone

State is carried by shape, weight or words as well as colour: a selected Team Card takes a 2px line as well as the colourway, an interstate tag is an outline and names the city, and a match outside NSW has a dashed ring.

Focus

  • Every control shows a 2px focus ring in --c-focus, offset 2px, on keyboard focus only (:focus-visible).
  • Focus rules meant for the keyboard must not catch a tap: a tapped button keeps focus, so check behaviour after a tap as well as after a key.
  • Closing anything returns focus to what opened it. See page movement and focus.

Reduced motion

With reduced motion on, movement is swapped for a fade or an instant change. Nothing that carries meaning depends on motion alone.

Screen readers

  • Every change is announced once. Do not make a toast a live region when the change is already announced.
  • Buttons that toggle use aria-pressed; the visible label can be short, with the full meaning in visually hidden text (for example "Add Bondi to Coogee walk to your plan").
  • Opening a level of a sheet announces "[name], details opened." and going back announces "Back to [title]."
