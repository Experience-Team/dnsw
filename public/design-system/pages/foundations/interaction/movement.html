Movement can be followed

Anything that moves because of a tap or click glides, so the eye can track it from start to end. For example, a picked card travels to the middle of the fan rather than appearing there.

  • Check motion frame by frame, not only where it ends up.
  • A front-loaded curve such as expo-out covers most of the distance in the first few frames and reads as a jump. Use --ease-out or --ease-in-out from the motion tokens.

The movement that matters is obvious

A change people should notice must be clear at real size and speed. When a whole group moves together (a fan turning, a list shifting), the item the person acted on gets lost, so single it out: lift it, scale it, bring it above its neighbours, then settle it.

Judge it from a strip of frames at real size.

Accept it because the numbers prove it moved.

Reduced motion

Slides and scales become fades, or swap in place. Durations drop to the shortest fade or to nothing. Meaning never depends on motion.
