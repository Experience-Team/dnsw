Typefaces

Three roles. In the Wireframe they are all the system font. The Third Half uses IBM Plex as a stand-in for Social Condensed and Social Mono until Landor supplies the final fonts.

  • --font-body: running text, buttons, cards.
  • --font-display: headings and the slogan.
  • --font-label: short caps labels only (team codes, tags, the hero kicker). Never sentences.

Type scale

Body text in product is 16px or more. Samples use the brand's body face.

Weights

The Third Half sets all three heavy weights to SemiBold: its headings and labels are 600.
