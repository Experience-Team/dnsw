Short and smooth. Press Play to see each one at real speed. With reduced motion on, these become fades or instant changes.

Durations

Exits run at --dur-exit-factor of the entry's duration, so things leave a little faster than they arrive.

Easing

Use --ease-out for things arriving and --ease-in-out for things moving from one place to another.

Use an expo-out curve: it covers most of the distance in the first frames and reads as a jump.
