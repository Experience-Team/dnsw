Choices behave like what they mean

An "all", "any" or "none" option replaces specific picks, and picking a specific item replaces it. The two never mix.

Picking "Anything" turns off Beaches and Food and drink. Picking Walks turns "Anything" off.

Show "Anything" and "Beaches" both on.

Every multi-select can be cleared

When people can pick several things, give them one action to clear them all and start again. Show it only when something is picked.

  • Clear all can end a caption's sentence as a text link ("3 teams picked. Clear all").
  • After clearing, focus moves to the first option, because Clear all has gone.

Summaries describe the latest change

Make several changes in a row and check every status or summary line describes the latest one, not the one before.

Single choice

For one-of-a-set, picking one replaces the last. See chips.
