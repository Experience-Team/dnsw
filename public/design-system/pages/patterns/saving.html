No account needed

A plan is kept on the device and works fully without signing in. Ask people to sign in only when they want to keep or share it across devices.

Say plainly where it is kept: "Your plan is currently saved to this device only."

Taking it off the site

Research shows fans rarely use save functions and keep plans in notes apps, spreadsheets or on paper. So the plan leaves the site easily:

  • Copy as text
  • Add to calendar
  • Download or print
  • Share a link

If the clipboard is blocked, show the text in a box to copy from.

Where it lives

Save & share is the brand button at the foot of the plan. It opens a stacked sheet with these options and nothing else.

Optional extras come after

A news opt-in is optional and comes after the action, quieter than the form it follows.
