  1. 1The selector shows Japan. One team for the whole page: changing it here, in the planner sentence or in the match filter changes the other two. Clear team resets the page to the default and forgets the team.
  2. 2Supporting bar: stays under the header while scrolling. Change scrolls back to the selector and opens it. Clear resets the page to the default, as before choosing a team.
  3. 3Countdown switches to Japan's first NSW match.
  4. 4Matches in NSW comes first, filtered to Japan. Show all 17 matches clears the filter.
  5. 5Plan your 3rd half, with Japan and their NSW matches already filled in.
  6. 6Find your people: where Japan fans meet, watch and celebrate. Up to four parts; each only shows when there is content for the team. Compact: two parts side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile.
  7. 7Country house: run by each nation, so content depends on the team or its union. Details marked To be confirmed until announced.
  8. 8Where fans watch: 3 venues, All venues, and How venues are listed. Neutrality rules for listing venues need DNSW sign-off.
  9. 9Team moments: dated items (arrival, open training, community events) with date, time and place. To be confirmed where needed.
  10. 10One line linking out to the team's official supporters' club.
  11. 11Stadiums where Japan play come first. The others are collapsed under Other stadiums. The Tumbalong Park fan zone always stays visible.
