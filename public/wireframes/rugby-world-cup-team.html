Rugby World Cup page: choose your team

Fans choose their team once, near the top. The page then shows that team's content first; the general content stays, lower down. Every team gets a useful page, including fans visiting Sydney without tickets who want fan zones and watch parties. Shown before a team is chosen, with South Africa (no NSW matches), with Japan (some matches in NSW, some elsewhere), and with Japan when the fan is watching rather than going.

  1. 1The selector shows Japan, labelled Plays in Newcastle in the dropdown. One team for the whole page: changing it here, in the planner sentence or in the match filter changes the other two. Clear team resets the page to the default and forgets the team.
  2. 2Supporting bar: stays under the header while scrolling. Change scrolls back to the selector and opens it. Clear resets the page to the default, as before choosing a team.
  3. 3Countdown switches to Japan's first NSW match.
  4. 4Japan’s matches: all three pool matches in date order, in Sydney time. The NSW match shows its venue and Getting to the stadium. The other two say where they're played, with Watch in NSW links to the fan zone and Where Japan fans watch.
  5. 5A live match to see: Japan have picked Sun 3 Oct in the planner, so the next NSW match after that is Fiji v Spain, the next day, also in Newcastle.
  6. 6Show all 17 matches in NSW switches to the full NSW list. No possible path line: Japan already play in NSW.
  7. 7Plan your 3rd half defaults to going to for teams with NSW matches, with their NSW match picked. The matches part reads as dates.
  8. 8Find your people: where Japan fans meet, watch and celebrate. Up to four parts; each only shows when there is content for the team. Compact: two parts side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile.
  9. 9Country house: run by each nation, so content depends on the team or its union. Details marked To be confirmed until announced.
  10. 10Where fans watch: 3 venues, All venues, and How venues are listed. Neutrality rules for listing venues need DNSW sign-off. The Watch in NSW links in the match rows land here.
  11. 11Team moments: dated items (arrival, open training, community events) with date, time and place. To be confirmed where needed.
  12. 12One line linking out to the team's official supporters' club.
  13. 13Stadiums where Japan play come first. The others are collapsed under Other stadiums. The Tumbalong Park fan zone always stays visible.
