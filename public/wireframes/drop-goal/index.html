On the West route the Blue Mountains are solid: a low, flat kick hits the face and rolls back to the foothills, so you need height to reach Bathurst and beyond. The km estimate under Power assumes no wind.

Prototype. Destinations are placeholders in real distance order from the start point. Local covers roughly the first 30 km; the other routes start beyond it so stops are not repeated. Each stop's landing zone is sized from 4,000 simulated kicks so that every stop catches roughly 1 in 10 kicks, which is why some zones are wider than others. Distances are approximate. Clearing the crossbar is a bonus; every kick lands somewhere.
