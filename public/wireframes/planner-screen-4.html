Places 3 to 5 and "Not found", same cards
  1. 1Title and intro. Fans reach this page from "Already have a plan? Check it against your dates" on the set-up page.
  2. 2The same set-up sentence, cut down to team, matches, dates and city. Dates default to the span of the chosen matches.
  3. 3Where the fan is staying. Needed for the "far from your stay" check.
  4. 4Search: type a name, pick from real listings, Add. Matches on listing title (live).
  5. 5Or paste a list from notes or a chat. Each line is matched to a listing, and a day or time in the line ("Monday", "Saturday lunch") is used for the check. To confirm: how lines are matched to listings.
  6. 6Summary of the checks. Places that need a change come first.
  7. 7Closed on the day the fan listed. Suggests a day that works, with "Use this day". Opening hours come from availability (gap).
  8. 8Clashes with a match, allowing for travel time to the stadium. Match times are not in contract.
  9. 9Far from where they're staying, with the travel time and a free day it suits. Travel time is not in contract.
  10. 10Fits well: the reason the check passed, in one line.
  11. 11Also on nearby: a fan zone screening or event near this place on that day, with a button to add it as well.
  12. 12Not found: a line we couldn't match. It can't be checked and won't go into the plan unless the fan finds it with search.
  13. 13Map: the fan's places, numbered to match the cards, plus both venues and both stays.
  14. 14Puts the places into the plan's gaps on their days, or on the suggested day where "Use this day" was chosen. Flags stay on the cards. "Not found" places are left out.
  15. 15Mobile: search and paste stack, the map is one tap away, and the cards are shorter.
