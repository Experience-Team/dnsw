Saturday evening and days 3 to 6, same pattern
  1. 1The set-up sentence, pinned at the top. Changing any part updates the plan; locked items stay.
  2. 2Take it with you: copy, download, print or share (screen 5, built later). Repeated at the bottom.
  3. 3Trip map: the whole stay, with both cities, where the fan is staying, both venues and the travel time between cities.
  4. 4Day header: date, day number and city. Match days are labelled.
  5. 5Where the fan is staying: an area or address they add (no booking). It's the start point for travel times. Not part of the set-up sentence.
  6. 6Empty state: without an arrival time there are no options for Friday evening yet.
  7. 7Gap header: length and location. After a choice it shows how much time is still free.
  8. 8A chosen option: "In your plan" with a time, plus swap, remove and lock. The other two stay as alternatives with "Add to plan".
  9. 9Also on: fan zone screenings and events near this gap on that date. From event listings; their dates and sessions are availability (gap).
  10. 10Match block, fixed. The travel time from the previous stop sits above it.
  11. 11The 3rd half: the evening after the final whistle, between the venue and the fan's stay.
  12. 12Between cities: the train leg sits in the timeline, with options on the way, often in regional NSW.
  13. 13Day map: stays in view while scrolling and follows the day on screen. Chosen stops in order, with travel times between them (not in contract). Shows a prompt when nothing is chosen.
  14. 14Take it with you, again at the end of the plan.
  15. 15Mobile: the timeline comes first. The trip map is one tap away.
  16. 16Mobile: "Show map" on each day opens that day's map in place, here open on day 2.
