# Version 1

Version 1 (was Option C until 25 Nov) is `../prototype-pick-your-team-v1-frame.html`, shown in the viewer as the first tab
(`../prototype-pick-your-team.html?version=1`). It runs on the shared scripts and styles (`../shared.css`, `../concepts-*.js`,
`../pv-device.js`), which Version 2 and a few other pages also load, so **nothing for Version 1 goes into a shared file**.
New behaviour is a file here, linked from Version 1's own page, that reads what the shared scripts draw and adds to it.
The same rule as `../v2/README.md`, the other way round.

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `v1-device.css` | Version 1's own page styles. |
| `v1-social.css`, `v1-social.js` | Sydney on social, Version 1's copy (Home, above the FAQs, and its viewer). |
| `v1-flow-motion.js`, `.css` | Moving through the questions (26 Nov 2026). Next, Skip and Show every NSW match **push**: a still of the step on show (the ghost, copied just before the flow redraws, with each part keeping the display it has) slides out the way the person is going while the real next step slides in, over the same distance and time (480ms, an even ease), inside the stage `#tp-pick`, which is the frame (clipped at its sides). Back is the mirror. Finishing the flow does not push. The cards **deal in** each time the hand arrives at step 1 (the Plan tab opened): they leave a pile at the foot of the hand and fan out (640ms, 17ms apart). It replaces the flow's own 32px slide and the shared one-off fade-in of the cards while it runs. Reduced motion: nothing moves. |
| `v1-flow-foot.js`, `.css` | Back in the pinned bar (26 Nov 2026). The flow draws Back at the top of each question; this copies it into the bar as the first button (a 36px square beside Skip and Next) after every redraw, and hides the one at the top. The copy keeps `data-fc-go="-1"`, so the flow's own handler and the push treat it as the original. |
| `v1-flow-c.js`, `v1-teams.js`, `v1-third-half.js`, `v1-plan.js`, `v1-map.js` (26 Nov 2026, questions and defects) | The tickets step and every I have tickets control are gone (the matches are inferred from the teams and the days; each has a quiet Remove, kept in `state.notGoing`, and comes back from Also on in NSW). Steps are your team, your days and where you're staying (required, one base, with This is my best guess in `state.trip.guess`). The Plays in NSW switch is gone and the NSW tag always shows. Show every NSW match means no team: nothing is inferred (`tlShownAll` is false for it), and the NSW matches inside the days are listed with Add under On while you're here. Nothing outside the days shows on the team cards (`inDates`). Find the fan zones replaces Watch in NSW; Add then Added everywhere; a close cross on the Your plan sheet and the Your answers level; Share and export options with a Share row (`navigator.share`, copy as the fallback); the plan's map pins only where a day's own things are; one list of a day's ideas (`dayIdeas`) for the plan's list and the full-screen map. |
| `v1-picker.css` | Team picker (26 Nov 2026): the hand has six cards to a row (`handGeo` in `v1-teams.js`), tighter rows so all 24 teams fit one screen; the hand is centred and may reach about 6px past each side; pressing and moving it turns every row about the centre of its arc (up to 7 degrees, `holdSet` in `v1-teams.js`) and it stays where it is let go (Grid and back to Fan centres it). Find and Grid are borderless 28px buttons with icons. The step's note no longer says No team? Show every NSW match. The peek script's focus rule no longer runs in Option C (tapping Grid or Find scrolled the page and swallowed the tap). |
| `v1-dates.css`, `v1-third-half.js`, `v1-flow-c.js` | Dates (26 Nov 2026): one open range calendar (the later rounds marked as diamonds), on the Your days step, in the Your days sheet and in Your answers; a read-only strip of the tournament sat above it briefly and was removed the same day as noise. The slider and the typed First day and Last day fields are gone. `v1-third-half.js` and `v1-flow-c.js` are Version 1's copies of `../concepts-third-half.js` and `../concepts-flow-c.js`, copied so no shared script is edited: the page loads these now, and nothing loads the originals. `v1-dates.css` holds the strip, the calendar marks, the step's list of picked matches and Also on in NSW. Your plan, new layout (26 Nov 2026): `v1-plan.js` and `v1-plan.css` (Version 1's copy of `../concepts-plan.js`) draw the plan as one vertical timeline on a rail (solid for days with something on, dotted for empty days), free days with a big plus and Suggest ideas that opens up to three ideas on the card (`tp.ideasInline` in `v1-third-half.js`), a List or Map toggle, and getting there and where, how to get there and when on the cards (`tp.goOne`, `tp.itemMore`). Fan zones and watch parties (26 Nov 2026): `v1-fanspots.js` holds the sample details (suburb or venue, transport, opening times, what is there), `v1-fanzones.js` and `v1-fanzones.css` build the Fan zones and fan villages list and the Pubs and fan zones map after Where to watch, and `v1-map.js` (Version 1's copy of `../concepts-map.js`) drops the venue pins from the planner's map and puts that map above the match cards. Also on in NSW (`renderAlso` in `v1-third-half.js`, `#v1-also` in the page) lists the other NSW matches inside the days and a Newcastle card when one fits. |
