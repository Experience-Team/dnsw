Explore New South Wales

NSW, Australia

Welcome to New South Wales, where coastal escapes meet country retreats, rainforest adventures and unforgettable experiences lie around every corner. Whether you're seeking wide open spaces, world-class food and wine, or exhilarating outdoor thrills, discover the places that make Sydney and regional NSW.

From the golden beaches of the South Coast to the vineyards of the Hunter Valley and the snow-capped peaks of the Snowy Mountains, NSW rewards every kind of traveller. Follow the coast, chase the sunrise from Byron Bay, or head inland to outback plains and heritage gold-rush towns. However you explore it, there's always somewhere new around the corner.

More about New South Wales

New South Wales is Australia's most visited state, and it's easy to see why. From the bright lights of Sydney to the vast red plains of outback NSW, from the alpine meadows of the Snowy Mountains to the lush subtropical rainforests of the Far North Coast — there really is no shortage of things to do and places to explore in this diverse and spectacular state.
