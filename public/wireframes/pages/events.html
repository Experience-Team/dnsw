FAQs

Sydney's events calendar is packed year-round. Use the date filter above to browse events happening today or this weekend, from free outdoor concerts and markets to ticketed shows and exhibitions.
Sydney's most celebrated annual events include Vivid Sydney (light festival), the Sydney Festival, New Year's Eve fireworks, Mardi Gras, and the Sydney Royal Easter Show.
Many of Sydney's best events are free — from open-air cinema and outdoor concerts to museum exhibitions and cultural festivals. Use the "Free" filter chip to discover no-cost options.
The CBD, Darling Harbour, the Domain, Walsh Bay Arts Precinct, and Newtown are Sydney's main event hubs. Sydney Olympic Park hosts major sports and concerts.
Opal Card public transport (trains, buses, ferries, light rail) covers most event venues. The Opal app shows real-time service info. Rideshare and taxi services are widely available for late-night events.
Public holidays and long weekends see a burst of activity across Sydney — markets, outdoor cinema, special exhibitions and family events ramp up. Filter by date to see what's on during specific periods.
Sydney Opera House, Enmore Theatre, Factory Theatre, Hordern Pavilion, and the Metro Theatre are among Sydney's most iconic live music venues, hosting everything from intimate gigs to international tours.
