Disney's The Lion King is is now playing at the Capitol Theatre!

Prepare for a spectacle far beyond your imagination as you follow the powerful story of Simba as he journeys from wide-eyed cub to his destined role as King of the Pridelands.

Seen by over 124 million people worldwide, this landmark musical combines breathtaking visual artistry, unforgettable music, and a deeply moving story that has captured hearts across generations.

Winner of six Tony Awards® including Best Musical, this is a spectacular theatrical experience you will never forget.
