Australia welcomes the world for Rugby World Cup 2027, and Sydney is at the heart of the action. From pool matches to knockout fixtures, catch the world's best teams at the city's iconic stadiums, then soak up the atmosphere at fan zones across the harbour.

    FAQs

    NSW hosts 17 matches from Sunday 3 October to the final on Saturday 13 November 2027, including pool matches, 2 round of 16 matches, both quarter-finals, both semi-finals, the bronze final and the final. Check Matches in NSW above for fixtures and kick-off times at each venue.
    Tickets are available through the official Rugby World Cup 2027 ticketing portal. Packages covering multiple matches and hospitality options are also on sale.
    Opal Card public transport (trains, buses and light rail) runs extra services to Stadium Australia, Sydney Football Stadium and Newcastle Stadium on match days. Ticket holders can use event transport included with some match tickets.
    Yes. The NSW fan zone is at Tumbalong Park in Darling Harbour, with big screens, food and drink stalls, live entertainment, merchandise and family-friendly activities. Smaller match-day zones at the stadiums are still to be confirmed. See Stadiums and fan zones above.
