More about accommodation

Accommodation in Sydney gives you the choice to immerse yourself in the city's vibrant culture, or retreat to a peaceful haven away from the buzz. Sydney's hotel scene ranges from sprawling five-star harbourside resorts to intimate boutique properties tucked into historic laneways. Wherever you choose to stay, you'll find world-class service and amenities that make your visit unforgettable.


For those seeking something a little more local, Sydney's bed and breakfast scene offers a warm, personal touch. Hosted by locals with a passion for their city, B&Bs across Paddington, Glebe and the Northern Beaches give travellers the chance to wake up to a home-cooked breakfast and insider tips on the best spots in town.

Budget-conscious travellers won't be left out either — Sydney has a strong offering of well-located hostels, serviced apartments and three-star hotels that deliver comfort without breaking the bank. Many are positioned within walking distance of major public transport links, making it easy to get around without needing to hire a car.


For a truly unique experience, consider glamping in the bush fringes of Sydney or staying in a heritage cottage in the Hawkesbury region. These alternative stays give you the chance to disconnect from the city and reconnect with the spectacular natural landscape that surrounds Greater Sydney — all without sacrificing a comfortable night's sleep.
