About Sydney West

Western Sydney spans Parramatta, Penrith, Campbelltown, Blacktown, Camden and the Southern Highlands fringe, 25–95km west of the CBD. Home to Sydney Olympic Park, Featherdale Wildlife Park and the NSW Rail Museum, the region opens direct via Western Sydney International Airport from October 2026.

Sydney West Neighbourhoods

Parramatta

Sydney's second city and one of Australia's oldest colonial settlements, packed with heritage sites, a buzzing food scene and riverside parklands.

Known for: history and heritage; multicultural food scene

Highlights:

  • Old Government House – Australia's oldest surviving public building
  • Parramatta Park – UNESCO-listed riverside parklands
  • Riverside Theatres – 700+ shows a year, from comedy to concerts
Learn more about Parramatta →

Cabramatta

Sydney's home of Vietnamese and South-East Asian food and culture, with bustling markets, street food and the ornate Pai Lau Friendship Arch.

Known for: Vietnamese food scene; Asian grocery markets

Highlights:

  • Pai Lau Friendship Arch
  • Freedom Plaza
  • Cabramatta Markets
Learn more about Cabramatta →

Sydney Olympic Park

Home of the 2000 Olympics, now a hub of stadiums, parklands and wetlands beside the Parramatta River.

Known for: major stadiums; wetlands and cycling trails

Highlights:

  • Accor Stadium
  • Bicentennial Park
  • Sydney Showground
Learn more about Sydney Olympic Park →

Blacktown

A fast-growing multicultural hub with Featherdale Wildlife Park, live sport and one of Sydney's biggest regional shopping precincts.

Known for: Featherdale Wildlife Park; live sport at Blacktown Stadium

Highlights:

  • Featherdale Wildlife Park
  • Blacktown Showground
  • Nurragingy Reserve
Learn more about Blacktown →

Campbelltown

A place full of historical charm, lively entertainment and natural beauty. Enjoy the surrounds on a bushwalk, mountain bike ride or river kayak, and discover contemporary art, Indigenous and colonial heritage, and Australia's largest botanic garden.

Known for: history and heritage; live entertainment

Highlights:

  • Campbelltown Arts Centre
  • Japanese Gardens
  • Australian Botanical Garden
Learn more about Campbelltown →

Penrith

Find a mix of history, art and the outdoors in the action-packed Western Sydney suburb of Penrith. Enjoy a line-up of great eateries, cultural treasures at museums and galleries, and extreme thrills like skydiving, whitewater rafting and wakeboarding — all within an hour's drive from Sydney, at the foot of the Blue Mountains, on the spectacular Nepean River.

Known for: indoor skydiving, whitewater adventure and Blue Mountains access

Highlights:

  • iFLY Downunder indoor skydiving
  • Penrith Whitewater Stadium
  • Nepean River foreshore
Learn more about Penrith →

Thirlmere

A slice of Southern Highlands charm on Sydney's fringe, best known for its heritage steam trains and quiet lakeside walks.

Known for: NSW Rail Museum; heritage steam train rides

Highlights:

  • NSW Rail Museum
  • Thirlmere Lakes National Park
  • Steam Fest (seasonal)
Learn more about Thirlmere →

Appin

A small rural village on the Georges River, surrounded by bushland trails and historic farmland on the way to the Southern Highlands.

Known for: bushwalking trails; rural heritage

Highlights:

  • Georges River walking tracks
  • Appin Lookout
  • Cataract Dam
Learn more about Appin →

Camden

Sydney's colonial farming heartland, with heritage cottages, vineyards and paddocks now edging the new Western Sydney Airport precinct.

Known for: colonial heritage; vineyards and farm gates

Highlights:

  • Camden Park House & Garden
  • Local vineyards
  • Camden Show (seasonal)
Learn more about Camden →

Sydney West FAQs

Spring and autumn offer the mildest weather for exploring outdoors, though many attractions like Featherdale Wildlife Park and Sydney Olympic Park's wetlands are enjoyable year-round.
The T1 Western Line and T2 Inner West & Leppington Line connect the CBD to Parramatta, Blacktown, Penrith and Campbelltown, with local buses covering the surrounding suburbs.
