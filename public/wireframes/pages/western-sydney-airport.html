Western Sydney International (Nancy-Bird Walton) Airport is Sydney's newest international gateway, located at Badgerys Creek in Sydney's west. Built to serve the fast-growing Western Sydney region, it sits around 45km from the CBD and is designed to operate 24 hours a day. Find out more.

International Flights

Western Sydney International's terminal handles flights linking Sydney's west to destinations across Asia, the Pacific and beyond. Check your ticket for terminal and gate information, as airlines and routes continue to be added as the airport ramps up services. Customs queues may delay your exit, so check what you can and cannot bring into Australia on the Australian Border Force website.

Domestic Flights

Alongside international services, Western Sydney International also operates a growing range of domestic routes connecting Sydney's west to major cities and regional NSW. Airlines using the airport include Qantas, Jetstar and Virgin Australia, with additional regional carriers expected as schedules expand.

Getting between terminals

Unlike Sydney (Kingsford Smith) Airport's separate domestic and international buildings, Western Sydney International is built around a single integrated terminal. International and domestic flights are processed from the same building via different zones, so there's no shuttle bus or transfer needed between terminals.

Getting to and from Western Sydney International airport

Western Sydney International is about 45km from the Sydney CBD and is easily accessible by train, bus or car.

Train

The new Sydney Metro – Western Sydney Airport line runs directly into the terminal, connecting to St Marys station where you can change onto the T1 North Shore & Western Line for the city and beyond. You can use an Opal Card (available to purchase at the station) or a contactless Amex, Visa or Mastercard to pay for your fare. Find out more about Sydney Metro trains and fares.

Bus

Public bus services connect the airport to surrounding suburbs including Liverpool, Penrith and Campbelltown, with routes timed to align with metro and flight schedules.

Western Sydney International airport shuttle

Shuttle bus and limousine services are available to and from the terminal. You can make reservations at information desks in the arrivals hall or online prior to your arrival.

Car

Driving from the CBD to the airport takes about 45 minutes in light traffic via the M4 and M12 motorways. Short and long-stay parking is available at the terminal and is generally cheaper if pre-booked. Valet and accessible parking is also available. There are various passenger pick-up and drop-off zones — for details, check out Western Sydney International's website.

Taxi and ride share

Taxis are available from organised taxi ranks directly outside the terminal. Rideshare services including Uber and Ola pick up from the priority pick-up areas.

Western Sydney International airport shopping and restaurants

Western Sydney International has a variety of shops, restaurants and cafes across the terminal. Baggage storage and currency exchange are available, and you'll find a range of local and national retailers at the airport. Find out more about shopping at Western Sydney International.

Western Sydney International airport duty free

Duty free is available at the international terminal after security. Allowances and limits apply.

Travel security

The Australian Government's travel security website TravelSECURE is an online resource which provides information on the security measures in place at Australian airports. Its purpose is to help travellers prepare for their journey and clear the security checks at the airport quickly and easily.
