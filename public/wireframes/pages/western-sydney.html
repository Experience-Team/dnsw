Sydney's fast-growing western suburbs stretch from the historic streets of Parramatta to the rolling foothills of the Blue Mountains. Home to a thriving multicultural food scene, Cathedral (Aboriginal) rock art, Auburn Botanic Gardens and Sydney Olympic Park, and the World Heritage-listed Greater Blue Mountains, this is where Australia's next chapter is being written — while wide open spaces around Camden and Campbelltown showcase the countryside's charming past.
