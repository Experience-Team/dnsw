How we choose options

Every option is a real listing on sydney.com or visitnsw.com. We choose them by:

  • Distance: how close they are to your previous stop or where you're staying
  • Opening: open on your dates, at the time of the gap
  • Interests: the interests in your set-up sentence

Fidelity review: all planner screens

Every planner page was checked against the fidelity rules in planner-brief.md: the components page, screens 1 to 6, and the planner section on the Rugby World Cup page.

RuleWhat the review foundChange made
Greyscale onlyOpening hours (a gap field in the data contract) had a faint pink wash, the only colour on any planner screen.Changed to a faint grey wash inside planner screens, so the field still reads as provisional.
No iconsThe "All wireframes" links on every planner page started with an arrow symbol.Arrow removed.
Plain system font, 2 or 3 sizesField names in the notes (such as availability) were in a code font at a smaller fourth size.Now plain system font at the note size. The planner CSS uses only 1.25rem, 1rem and .8125rem.
If in doubt, simplerTwo placeholders on the components page had one-off inline heights.Replaced with the shared short placeholder style.
No gradients or shadowsNone found. The X in each placeholder is a drawn line (SVG), and markers use an outline, not a shadow.No change.
Images and maps as grey boxes with an X and a labelAll images and maps follow this, including the print preview's map.No change.
Placeholder text, no real business namesOnly "Team A", "Venue name", "Place name" and similar. Real names are limited to cities, sydney.com and visitnsw.com, and Google Maps, which the brief names.No change.
Numbered markers and side notesEvery section on every screen has markers with notes beside it (or below it on mobile).No change.
Kept on purposeThe small down-arrow on multi-choice slots matches the arrow the browser draws on the other dropdowns. The desktop day map on screen 2 really does follow the scroll, to show the "stays visible" requirement.Kept. Easy to remove if the UI designer prefers.
