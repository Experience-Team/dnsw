Pick your team: Version 1

Version 1: the Plan tab asks the questions up front, one step at a time: your teams, your days and where you’re staying. All three are required, and the plan does not open until all three are answered. A test from team feedback, with its own saved picks.

Phone size only. Places are real; their hours, prices, screenings and the fan zone and village sites are indicative. Pink marks data not yet confirmed.

        Six weeks. Two host cities.

        Matches run from 1 October to 13 November 2027 in Sydney and Newcastle, with 9 pool matches and the knockouts in NSW.

        Leave the car at home.

        Trains run from Central to Olympic Park in about 30 minutes, and Moore Park is a walk from Central through Surry Hills. Sydney to Newcastle is just under three hours by train.
