  1. 1"Take it with you" in the plan view (top and bottom) opens the panel.
  2. 2"Copy details" on every option card copies one place: name, address, hours on that day and a link. A one-line confirmation shows what was copied.
  3. 3The panel opens beside the plan; Close goes back. Every option works without an account.
  4. 4Copy as text: plain text with days, times, place names, addresses and links, ready to paste into notes or a chat.
  5. 5Download spreadsheet (CSV): one row per stop, matches included. Columns: day, time, place, address, opening hours, travel time, link.
  6. 6Print or save as PDF: one page with a simple map, times and a tick box next to each stop.
  7. 7Add to calendar: each stop and match becomes an event (.ics file).
  8. 8Open in Google Maps: one day at a time, as a route through that day's stops.
  9. 9Copy share link: the plan is stored in the link itself, so no account is needed and nothing is saved on our side. Opening it gives a copy; changes don't affect the sender's plan.
  10. 10Mobile: the sheet comes up from the bottom over the plan. Previews are shorter, and only the text preview shows.
